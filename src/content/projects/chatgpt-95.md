A love letter to bad UI design. ChatGPT-95 wraps a ChatGPT interface in the full Windows 95 experience — window chrome, pixel fonts, the works. It shouldn't work as well as it does.

Built in TypeScript as a weekend project and shipped open source. The reactions from people who grew up with Windows 95 are consistently either "this is delightful" or "why would you do this." Both are valid.
